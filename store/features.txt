Edit, Split and Preview layouts (Ctrl+1 / Ctrl+2 / Ctrl+3); in Split the two panes scroll as one, and they stack when the window is narrow.
Zen mode: full screen, your text in one centred column, a quiet switch between writing and reading.
Live preview of headings, lists, task lists, tables, quotes, code, links, images, footnotes and YAML or TOML front matter.
LaTeX math and mhchem chemistry, typeset on your PC by the bundled KaTeX engine.
Mermaid, Graphviz and PlantUML diagrams, drawn offline by engines built into the app.
Plot blocks: charts md draws itself from a formula, a parametric curve or a list of points — no engine behind them, a real vector in the page.
Syntax-highlighted code in a quiet palette that matches the page rather than a bright editor theme.
CSV and TSV blocks drawn as tables, the first row the header and number columns lined up on the right.
The Go menu lists every heading and your private author notes; a live word and character count sits under every page.
Every document reopens in the layout you last had it in.
Print through a print preview inside the window, or export real pages as PDF at A4, A5, US Letter, US Legal or a paperback trim size.
Export as one self-contained HTML file, a standard EPUB 3, a LaTeX .tex file, a TextBundle, or a single diagram as an SVG vector.
Writer mode: a folder is a book — subfolders are chapters, Markdown files are articles — compiled to one PDF, EPUB or LaTeX book.
Autosave in place a second after you stop typing, with a guard that asks before overwriting a file another program changed.
Opens .md, .markdown, .txt, .puml and .gv files and saves them back in the encoding they arrived in; opens .textpack files and TextBundle folders too.
Rename, Move To, Duplicate and Revert to Saved from the File menu; Open Recent feeds the taskbar Jump List.
Warm paper in light and dark, prose in Lucida Sans Typewriter and code in Courier New.
Built-in examples, including a small sample book.
No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no ads; the only network use is an image your own document links by URL.
Built for Windows 11 on x64 and ARM64.
